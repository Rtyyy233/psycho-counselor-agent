Working Alliance Inventory
"The Working Alliance Inventory: Supervisee and Supervisor Forms"
provides useful tools to evaluate alliance factors in the supervisory relation-
ship. Alliance and relationship factors are TASK, BOND, and GOAL,
which define agreement from the supervisor and the supervisee's perspective
on the tasks of supervision, the supervisory bond, and the goals of supervision.
The forms are conceptually sound and are useful in enhancing communica-
tion and strengthening the supervisory alliance. However, until additional
reliability and validity data are accumulated, they should not be used as
definitive measures.
